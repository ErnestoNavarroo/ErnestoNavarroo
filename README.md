### 🔧 Building now

**[robot-dog](https://github.com/ErnestoNavarroo/robot-dog)** — quadruped built at
EspaiTec (UJI). Current state: one leg under firmware control, on the bench.

- ESP32 driving STS serial-bus servos over a 1 Mbaud link, synchronised writes.
- Closed-chain four-bar leg: abduction, femur/tibia triangle, then the crank-and-rod
  solve for the ankle.
- Analytical inverse kinematics with reach and joint-limit checks, commanded live
  from a serial console (`x y z` in mm).
